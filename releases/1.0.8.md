# Guanaco 1.0.8

Released September 23, 2026.

Guanaco 1.0.8 restores live liquidity pools that could remain incorrectly
excluded by stale browser state.

## Improvements

- A complete market synchronization is now authoritative when rebuilding the
  available liquidity catalog.
- Pools confirmed as live are restored to market totals and swap routing.
- Historical exclusions that are not present in the live catalog remain
  preserved.
- Stale local state can no longer continue reducing the liquidity considered
  for pricing after a complete synchronization.

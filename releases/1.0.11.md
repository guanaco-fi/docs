# Guanaco 1.0.11

Released September 23, 2026.

Guanaco 1.0.11 prevents stale browser liquidity state from silently producing
suboptimal swap quotes.

## Improvements

- If any pool in a market may be stale, the entire affected market is
  temporarily withheld from pricing and routing.
- Guanaco refreshes only the affected market and restores it after receiving an
  authoritative liquidity snapshot.
- Partial market liquidity is never used to produce a quote while recovery is
  in progress.
- Unknown token values are displayed instead of misleading zero values during
  recovery.
- Unrelated market state remains available and is not cleared.

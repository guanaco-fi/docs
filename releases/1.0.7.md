# Guanaco 1.0.7

Released September 22, 2026.

Guanaco 1.0.7 prevents market synchronization from combining stale and current
liquidity data into an inconsistent catalog.

## Improvements

- Stale cached pages are no longer accepted as successful market discovery
  results when current data cannot be retrieved.
- Market synchronization now fails safely instead of publishing a catalog
  assembled from inconsistent data.
- Previously cached catalogs created under the older behavior are discarded
  and rebuilt from current liquidity data.

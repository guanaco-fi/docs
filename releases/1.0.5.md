# Guanaco 1.0.5

Released September 22, 2026.

Guanaco 1.0.5 prevents incomplete liquidity synchronization results from
temporarily replacing the complete market catalog.

## Improvements

- When market discovery is incomplete, Guanaco keeps the last complete
  liquidity snapshot instead of publishing a truncated market catalog.
- A new browser session waits for a complete market snapshot rather than
  presenting partial liquidity as live.
- A later complete synchronization replaces the previous snapshot normally.

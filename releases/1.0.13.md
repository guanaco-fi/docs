# Guanaco 1.0.13

Released September 23, 2026.

Guanaco 1.0.13 refreshes the Liquidity Markets dashboard for faster scanning
and a clearer view of how liquidity is distributed.

## Improvements

- BCH market totals now use consistent two-decimal formatting.
- Each market displays its Guanaco and Cauldron liquidity distribution in a
  compact visual bar.
- Protocol colors are consistent between individual markets and the global
  protocol overview.
- Market columns, identifiers, and copy controls have improved alignment and
  spacing across desktop and mobile layouts.
- Long market identifiers use a compact preview while preserving the complete
  value in the tooltip and copy action.
- The live-liquidity discovery and retry states provide clearer progress and
  recovery feedback.
- Market-row hover feedback no longer moves or clips table content.

# Guanaco 1.0.6

Released September 22, 2026.

Guanaco 1.0.6 ensures that browsers do not reuse incomplete liquidity catalogs
created by earlier versions.

## Improvements

- Previously cached market catalogs that may be incomplete are discarded and
  rebuilt from current liquidity data.
- Stale synchronization checkpoints are cleared so affected browsers can
  reconstruct the complete market catalog.
- Complete cached liquidity remains available when a later synchronization
  attempt is incomplete.

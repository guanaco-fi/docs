# Guanaco 1.0.9

Released September 23, 2026.

Guanaco 1.0.9 restores live liquidity pools that could remain unavailable due
to stale temporary browser-session state.

## Improvements

- Complete market synchronization now reconciles both persistent and
  session-specific liquidity exclusions.
- Pools confirmed as live are restored to token pricing and swap routing.
- Unrelated historical exclusions remain preserved.
- Valid token markets no longer remain unavailable after their pools have been
  confirmed by a complete synchronization.
